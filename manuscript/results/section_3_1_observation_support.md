### 3.1 Observation support and lake-specific proxy evidence

The two-lake experiments differed in both observational support and evaluation target (Figure 1; Table 1). Erken provided a dense chlorophyll-fluorescence reference (CHLF) for evaluating temporal reconstruction under actual Sentinel-2 sampling, whereas Vombsjön provided observed satellite MCI for a locked transfer test. The satellite–chlorophyll comparisons supplied separate, lake-specific observed-proxy evidence. These evidence streams therefore addressed different aspects of reconstruction and proxy interpretation.

Across 2019–2025, Erken had 307 Sentinel-2-usable dates, of which 288 supplied sparse reconstruction inputs after intersection with open-water conditions and available reference measurements. These inputs covered seven years and retained CHLF as both the reconstructed quantity and the evaluation reference. The actual observation mask and secondary controlled-missingness experiments consequently evaluated temporal reconstruction of the same variable. Reconstruction common support was boundary-truncated in 2019 and 2025, limiting interpretation of their evaluated maxima to the available support.

The separate Erken observation-layer comparison contained 220 exact-date MCI–CHLF pairs and 215 NDCI–CHLF pairs on processor-shared common-support calendars. These counts describe satellite-index matchups and are distinct from the 288 reconstruction inputs. ACOLITE MCI was positively associated with CHLF, with Spearman ρ = 0.458 and a 95% whole-year cluster-bootstrap interval of 0.295–0.610. Processor-specific MCI and NDCI associations, including the overlapping MCI intervals for L1C, L2A and ACOLITE, are reported in the Supplementary Information. This association provided lake-specific evidence connecting the observed MCI proxy to chlorophyll variability; it did not establish an absolute chlorophyll calibration or spatial and depth equivalence between satellite and in situ observations.

For Vombsjön, the independent processor calendars contained 335 ACOLITE, 419 L2A and 408 L1C observed dates during 2017–2026. ACOLITE was the frozen primary transfer processor, L2A a separate sensitivity and L1C a diagnostic baseline. Their fixed nominal-station 3×3 MCI series supported 1,200, 1,536 and 1,492 withholding scenarios, respectively (Table 1). These calendars were independent and were neither pooled nor used to rank processors. ACOLITE support in 2026 extended only through 3 August. The primary quantitative transfer target was withheld observed ACOLITE MCI.

Complementary Vombsjön field evidence comprised 54 field dates in 2018–2020, with seven valid fixed-polygon exact-date ACOLITE pairs and nine each for L2A and L1C. The ACOLITE pairs comprised four in 2019 and three in 2020; no processor had a valid 2018 pair. Paired ACOLITE field concentrations spanned approximately 1.206–33.9 µg L⁻¹, whereas the complete field dataset extended to approximately 125.7 µg L⁻¹. Thus, the paired evidence did not cover the 2018 extreme regime. Pair-level records are reported in Supplementary Table S5; field Chl-a supplies complementary proxy-consistency context, not daily reconstruction truth.

Erken therefore provides the dense-reference reconstruction experiment, while Vombsjön provides a locked withheld-satellite transfer test with complementary but sparse field context.
